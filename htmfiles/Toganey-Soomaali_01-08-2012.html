Toganey Soomaali (Soomaali Togan), 01-08-2012

Maansadan aan magaca uga dhigay Soomaali-togan ama Toganey Soomaali, waxay soo baxday 01 August 2012, waxayna tilmaamaysaa in yar oo ka mida cilladaha soomaali badankeedu nolosha kaga dhacday, oo ay ka mid yihiin: - inay Wanaaggii loo tilmaamo ka maahsantahay oo aanuu warkii fiicani gelayn, shartana si fudud u qaataan. - inay Nicmadii alle siiyey isku xasuuqdoo qaxooti ka dooratay. - Maahmaahyada diin la'aanta ah ee ay culummo & caammaba isticmaalaan oo ay ka mid yihiin AFAR &TOBANKA & BOQOLKAAN AQAAN KAA AQRISAN MAYO BAA KA ADAG & Tuug, tuug u lahaw, & Geel lama kala laha ee Xeraduu markaas ku jiraa iska leh, & weliba ninkii la yiri war Alle ka baq oo joogso oo ninka ha dilin, ee yiri isii daa, Alle ka baq & rag iska dhici meel islama galaane, aan ragga iska dhiciyee, yacni waa in uusan Alle ka baqaynin oo uu caddaystay, - in ay dalkoodii Alle siiyey ee qaaliga ahaa qaxooti ka doorteen oo wasiir dhami safarka uu fursadda u helo isku dhiibayo. - is xaqiraadda & isla weynida doqonnimo, oo aan haba yaraatee wax sababa lahayn. - in qabiil walbaa isbaaro agoonta & musaafurka lagu dhaco oo magacooda loogu yeero leeyahay, oo ay culummo & caamaba raalli ka yihiin, ciddii ku doodaysa qaar aan raalli ka ahayn baa jirana waxan ka doonayaa inay keento guryo la yaqaanno oo xoolaha ay qalaan ama ducada ay qabaan aan la cunin, amaba xitaa shaqsiyaad la yaqaanno oo gurigaas tegiddiisa diiday illeen kii tegey wax wuu ka cuniye, waa maya oo xitaa sheekooyinka caadiga ah & war badnida soomaalida meelna loogama soo daro, sidoo kale ma jiro qof suntan oo la yiri hebel xoolihiisu xalaal maaha. - in aabbe abtiriskiisa ilaa nabi aadanna ha gaaree gar & gar darroba naf & maal loo huro, hooyadii qabiilka odaygaas la dhashayna aan iyada magaceeda xitaa la aqoon. - Gabdhaha aan qaan gaarin ee waalidkii leeyahay orod tahriib ee lacag degdeg noogu soo dir taas oo aan dan & heello laga lahayn meeshay ka keenayso. - Musaafurka habaryarta & xitaa ayeyda lagu kufsado ee aan cidna ka diirayn. Soomaalidu ihaano jecelaa oo dhan xun wax ka eegi ogaa, Khushuuc la'aa oo Ad-adakaa, Qallafsanaa oo Dabci xumaa, Tawbad la'aa oo Nafteed halligsanaa, Hantideeda necebaa oo hanti dadow jecelaa, Nabadda shar ka jecelaa, Doorkeeda dili ogaa, Doqon islaameedna u nacamlayn ogaa, Halyeygeeda xaqiri ogaa doqon islaameedse ammaani ogaa, Diin & Dal & ehelkeedba necebaa oo Allaheedii abuurtay qabyaalad ka jecelaa, xalaal necebaa oo il-caddeeye(1) jecelaa, laaluush & musuq jecelaa, miskiin u xoog sheegashadi jecelaa, Marras ku tuma ogaa Marsoobaxse ka gariiri ogaa, Mooryaan u sujuudi ogaa, Wanaagga necebaa oo waawarey u taagnaan ogaa, Diin & xadiis quraafaad ka jecelaa, firkii aabbe caabudi ogaa oo kii hooyiyo ayeeyo dullaysan ogaa, magaceedu Reer Balaayo Hebel Balaayo, nacabkeedna reer khayr Ahlu-khayr, Waraabaha muhdigaa maxramkeed u loogi ogaa oo Ceebteed u soo taagnaan ogaa, Safarrey salaad ku gaadi ogaa, Ayeydeed musaafura ka sinaysan ogaa, ruux ka danqadase la'aa, Waayaha wax ku qaadasho la'aa, xusuusteeduna maranaa, Waagii yimaadaba wareer ku kordha jecelaa, Yaxaasyada caalamka masruufi ogaa, Halkii ay ku hoogtaba u sii hadoodilnaan ogaa, Kay korisaaba, iyaduu koroo, madaxaa ka toogtee, bal waa tan maansdadii: Tarbiyada islaamkee, is tusmayn wanaaggiyo, iska toosin xumahaan, tubteedii abbaariye, Soomalaay tusaalaha, tilmaamihi na hoojee, toogashada un noo qoray, farta baan ku taagiye, Tawaabkiinna eegoo, wixii run ahen toosiyo, Taam weeye ii dhaha, waxan xero taraaree, tolkey aan ku tacaddana, aan saxee i toosiya. 01- Tiro aan badnayn oo, toodu qarisay mooyee, Soomaali tumaneey, caqligeedu tabaneey. 02- Tii Khushuuca Taabkii, laga tiray Quluubtii, Luqunteedu toganeey, Tawbaddiina diiddaay. 03- Ruux siciisa turqayoo, saynta ugaga taaganoo, isagu is tarriiqeen, cidi toli kareyney. 04- Nimcaduu Tawaabkeed, Teel-teelka ugu dhigay, Gacmahaa ka taagtoo, Tuugsi uga dhaqaaqdaay. 05- Walaalkeeda taamkaa, Nasabnimo la too aho, taajkeeda weliba ah, Nolosheeda tiiriya, 06- Tabar & iyadoon, Taako qura an dheerayn, Jinni taladi raacdoo, xaqiraad ku taagtaay. 07- Diinta taababkeediyo, Tol middii illowdoo, Xaqiiqdii tallawdee, Dhan kaleeto taaganey. ba'aydiyo, waawarey la taah &, Cudurrada togaayiyo, Tubaal tii jeclaydey. 11- Taangiga dhacaayiyo, Jiibya taransetaatkiyo, Colaad taagan weligeed, Tuu caligu ka geynaay. 12- In Tawaabka laga baqo, in Rag layska tiiriyo, isma lehe i daa tiri, Toorrey la booddaay. 13- Afarya toban boqolaad, taqaan oo Qur'aankaa, kaa aqrisa maa tii, tiri oo ku faantaay. 14- wax ma taro wanaagguye, Tuug tuugo u lahaw, Tafiir jaraysaay. 17- Reer Tubaako beel &, reer Tookafeer &, magacyaal tahluukaa, ku Tafiir go'aysaay. 18- igu tiri ku teen aan, waxba aan tareyn &, turuufiyo khuraafaad, tii ku aayo seegtaay. 19- dhirteedii Tubaal &, Tarraq tii ku daartoo, isbaarada u taal &, tuugsiga ka jeceley. 20- Gabdhaheeda Timiriyo, Tigaad & Tisqaaddii, tii ka saartay aqalkee, ceebooba tiriyaay. 21- Toolmoonti quruxdiyo, Geed intay ku taagteen, tookaysta tiriyaay. 24- Tii aqalki joogtee, Tahriibkii ka diiddana, ciidammawgu tababbaran, teelaysta tiriyaay. 25- Toddobadeedi wiil oo, gabadheedi Toolmoon, kufsi uga tallaabsaday, tii ka diiri waysaay. 26- Toban jirka bikraad ee, Xoog loogu tegayoo, Talalkeedu yeerayo, taan u danqanaynaay. 27- Miskiintii la togayee, tuugo ay qabsaysee, Islaamow ku taahee, taan dheg jalaq u siinaay. 28- hadduu tuuggi u cawdee, Tol la'aayda ku yiraa, degdeg ugu tegeysoo, Tiknikada la duushaay. 29- tii Qabiil u teleftee, aabbaheed ab-tiriskiyo, Hooyadiis tu loo bogay, barta ay ku kala tegi, ku ekaysay Tolnimee, aabbaheeda Tala xume, Tirsigiya qabiilkiis, Tawbaddeeda mooddaay. - taasina ay keenee, qabaliyo taraarkeed, inay Caro la toostoo, tafaheedu dibaaxshaan. 30- Tafiirtii horeetiyo, Tu aan weli abuurrayn, yaa cadaawe ii tusay, igu talax tegsiinaya, marna tii an oraney. 31- Safarrey tubtoodiyo, Toobiyaha istaagaay, goortey tukanayaan, Tacaalooyin ku dhigaay, - tarankoy, Calal qura u taageyn, tooreeya tiriyaay, - Taa ruux dareemee, Jiririco tasooboo, Tallaabadu cuslaysana, tii aan ka waayaay . 32- Tamuuxday u dhalatiyo, Berrinkii Tagooggeed, Dulum tuu ka Taariikhdi hore &, Tagtadii illowdee, Timaaddada an garanoo, Berri aan tebeynaay. 38- Tubtii ay ku hoogtiyo, Tarab-tarab wixii baday, marna taan ka tegin ee, Tii uun ku joogtaay. 39- Koox aan la toyan ASBAABTA AY SIDAA KU MUTAYSATAY

Tusaalaha caddeyntiyo, daliishii ta'deydiyo, waxan Taab ka dhiganayo, iga hooda waa tane: 65- Turxaan laawe Rixinkiyo, Hooyada tafiirteed, goortay tufeen buu, Tawaabkey Raxiimkey, isagoo u turay uu, Quluubtii taxaaboo, Quful Tobanle seeraa, uga taagay laabtaa. 66- Dhegahoodu Tawbiyo, Tiraab khayr leh ma maqlaan, indhahoodu Tooskiyo, Tubta khayrka ma arkaan. 67- Ruuxa taasi diidee, taagaya fartiisow, bal adiguba taagtaa, Tusaalaaha Qur'aankiyo, axaadiista noo taal, & weli Tu sii dheer, intaad kula taloonbido, Car ha kaa tashiisheen. 68- Tii British ee beri, Teleefoonka duulaya, Booliis u tebisee, tiri SOOS boliisow, walax Timo madow &, Tufulaanful u egoo, Tafahana u dheer baa, ku tashaaya hoostoo, gidaarkeyga tuban oo, Halis baan ku taagnahay. 69- kolkey soo tawaaneen, halkuu Taarku ka yimina, Toos u soo abbaareen, labo hoos tuhaantuhey, waa Tarabbi Raagiyo, Talxe Geeddi Kaariye, Ta'doodii su'aashana, soo tuur Boliiskii, wuxu yiri bal tuugyahay, labo gaari Teetow, Reer madow Tukaalow, maxaa meeshan idin tubay ? 70- Tarabbibaa jawaab yiri, maya Taliye sheekiyo, Tamashlaan ku joognee, Tu kaleeto may jirin, Geeddina is tooxyoo, Tunka giir ka kiciyoo, Feer & Tatoomuu, isla soo turaanturay, Tol la'aay ayuu yiri, ma anoo Tahluukiyo, reer tuuga badan oo, Tobaneeyo dilay buu, Ingiriis tal-huumaa, i soo taaban karayaa ? 71- Taliskii ku bood oo, Dhul intey ku tuureen, kabo Taako dheer &, Tunka Buud ka saareen, tigtigeenna gebigood, bay yireen bal tuugyahay, Gaajahay tahriibkaa, maxaad tari karaysaa ?. 72- Halkii aad ku taranteed, Toltol aad ku ololisay, waxad uga timaadood, uga soo tahriibtana, inad Tiirarkaygiyo, Taababkayga siibtee, ninka tiisi soo gubay, ma tayduu u turayaa ? Hooyadiis Tiriig &, Tusantiisi uu dhalay, ninkii soo tahriibshee, Turriinshay ka waayeen, Taajartaa an dhalay &, Tayda uma naxaayee, yuu Turaabta teennaa, meel qura ka taabannin. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ 73- Eebbow Tawaabow, Taajirkii i uuntow, Tabya xoog ma waayow, siduu taamo yeelow, inkastood Taloog dheer, Taabab adag ka taagtood, tiri Tiimamkii an, tanaadkaan ka dhigay &, rogin Teebabkoodaa, kama taabto khaykaa, oo daliisha teeduna, Xattaa yugayir ay tahay, Ta'deeduna Anfaal &, Racdi ay ku taallana. 74- ha yeeshee Tawaabow, wuxu doono tire &, Eebbe taag leh baad tahay, waxa tawadinaysana, Cid ku toyan karaysoo, ku turtuuri may lahan. 75- inkastoon Tolkaygii, markan Eebbe tuugiyo, Taajirkiin iraahdaba, magacyaan teggaygiyo, Taariikhda ii gelin, ila tiigsanaayaan, 76- Tannaagadi midaa yiri, Itixaad ayaad tahay, Tol wahaabiyaad tahay, Awliyaad tiraysaa. 77- Tiraab kale mid baa yiri, suufi Teedki baad tahay, Tahliilaha tufaayiyo, Tusbaxlihi ayaad tahay. 78- mid kalaygu taagsaday, yiri reer Tagooriyo, reer Taako weyniyo, reer Talaado baad tahay. 79- anna Weli an tiro &, Tii wacanba daa ee, haddey Tayda noqon lahayd, Taladeey la qaadani, Gaaladaa turfaysiyo, Muslinkaa talluucaba, inay Nabad ku taamaan, Tu walbaan ka jecelahay. 80- Ciddii tooda eegtaba, waxa ay tiraahdaa, yaa magacan kaa tira, Qolo kalena kugu tola - ciddii Tawba eegtiyo, Taqilliyo Xaddiiskiyo, Ambiyadii tubtoodii, Samir bay tiraahdaa. 81- si kastaba Tawaabow, iyagoo i tuuryeyn, waxan taamina an hubin, misna gaajo Tiih &, Abaar looga tegay &, Qabiil baa ku talax tegoo, Teed baa u kala rogan, waa na Tii Muntina tay, wada daba turfaayaan, oo ay Tub khayrliyo, Toodii ku seegeen, 82- ee Allow Tawaabow, taagtaada adigoo, adeegsanaya naga tuur, Tiihiyo habaarkaan, Mirif noo tiraayoo, naga fuulay Tuurtaa.
